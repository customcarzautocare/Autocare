CUSTOMCARZ AUTO CARE — ADMIN V2 SETUP

1. Replace the repository's current admin.html with admin-v2.html (rename it to admin.html).
2. Upload customer-fix.js to the repository root.
3. In index.html, add this line immediately before </body>:
   <script src="customer-fix.js"></script>
4. Open admin.html.
5. Connect using:
   Owner: customcarzautocare
   Repository: Autocare
   Branch: main
   Token: a fine-grained GitHub token with Contents: Read and write.
6. Press REPAIR CATALOGUE STRUCTURE once, then SAVE/RELOAD.
7. Use Products & Sorting to drag products into the order you want and press SAVE PRODUCTS.
8. Use All Website Photos to replace the background, category photos and gallery photos.
9. Use Reviews to add/edit/remove reviews and SAVE ALL REVIEWS.

IMPORTANT:
- Product images are saved as relative paths under assets/products/.
- Category images are saved under assets/category/.
- Gallery images are saved under assets/gallery/.
- The customer site bridge reads site.json and products.json from this same Autocare repository.
- The bridge also makes Shop by Category work with the repaired main categories and shows all saved reviews.
- Do not paste your GitHub token into the files.
